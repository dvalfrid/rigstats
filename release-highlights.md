# 1.55.0
- **Now Playing on a screen of its own** — an extra monitor or a TV can show Now Playing filling the whole screen: the cover large over a soft, blurred copy of it, the title and artist, and the spectrum across the bottom (or around the cover). Choose the screen per Control Center profile on the Dashboard page, under Screen panels, whether it shows always or only while something plays, and whether it is a window or sits in the desktop background — there with a transparent background if you like, over your own wallpaper.
- **Disk temperature on more laptops** — a drive behind Intel's RST/VMD storage controller, common in business laptops, now shows its temperature in the Disk panel and can raise an alert. Found thanks to diagnostics from Neo, a beta tester — thank you!
- **The right logo on ASUS PCs** — an ASUS laptop or desktop that isn't a ROG (ExpertBook, Vivobook, Zenbook, TUF) now shows the ASUS logo in the dashboard header instead of ROG's.
