# 1.54.0
- **Now Playing panel** — a new optional panel shows what's playing: the cover, title, artist and album, and whether it plays or is paused, from Spotify, your browser or any player that tells Windows what it plays. No account or setup; turn it on in the Control Center's Dashboard page. What you listen to stays on screen: it isn't logged, recorded or put in a diagnostics file.
- **A spectrum in Now Playing** — bars that move with the sound below the cover, chosen per Control Center profile: Off, Calm or Smooth, in seven styles (bars, falling peaks, mirrored, neon lines, mountain, LED, dots) and in the theme's colour or a gradient (Dashboard page). Off by default; it listens and draws only while something plays and the panel is shown, so a profile for gaming can leave it off and it costs nothing there.
